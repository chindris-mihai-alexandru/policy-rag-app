# Acme Corp — Holiday Schedule 2026

**Policy ID:** ACME-HR-006
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2026
**Approved By:** Sarah Chen, VP of Human Resources
**Applies To:** All full-time and part-time employees of Acme Corp

---

## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the official company holidays observed by Acme Corp for the 2026 calendar year. On these days, Acme Corp offices are closed and employees are not expected to work, except for essential personnel as designated by their department.

---

---

## 3. Year-End Office Closure

In addition to the 12 official holidays, Acme Corp closes its offices during the **year-end shutdown period** from **December 26 through December 31, 2026**. This period is paid time off and does not count against employees' PTO balances.

The year-end shutdown applies to all employees except those designated as essential by their department heads. Essential employees who work during the shutdown period will receive **1.5x their regular pay rate** for hours worked, plus a compensatory day off to be taken within Q1 of the following year.

---

## 4. Floating Holidays

In addition to the official holidays, each full-time employee receives **2 floating holidays** per calendar year. Floating holidays provide flexibility for employees to observe days of personal, cultural, or religious significance not covered by the official holiday schedule.

### 4.1 Floating Holiday Guidelines

- Floating holidays are available starting January 1 each year (or prorated from the employee's start date for new hires).
- Floating holidays must be used within the calendar year and **do not carry over** to the following year.
- Floating holidays are **not paid out** upon separation from the company.
- Requests for floating holidays follow the same process as PTO requests (see PTO & Leave Policy, ACME-HR-001, Section 2.3).
- Floating holidays may be taken as full days or half days.

### 4.2 Common Uses for Floating Holidays

Employees commonly use floating holidays for (but are not limited to):

- Religious observances: Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Diwali, Yom Kippur, Rosh Hashanah, Lunar New Year, Good Friday, Orthodox Christmas
- Cultural celebrations: Lunar New Year, Nowruz, Día de los Muertos
- Personal milestones: Birthdays, anniversaries, family events
- State or local holidays not observed by Acme Corp

---

## 5. Religious Observance Accommodations

Acme Corp respects the religious beliefs and practices of all employees. Beyond the floating holidays, employees who need additional time off for religious observances may:

- Use PTO days
- Request unpaid leave for the observance day(s)
- Arrange a flexible schedule with their manager to make up the hours within the same work week

Requests for religious accommodations should be submitted to the employee's manager and HR. Acme Corp will make reasonable accommodations in accordance with applicable law.

---

## 6. Holiday Pay for Essential Personnel

### 6.1 Designation of Essential Personnel

Department heads are responsible for designating essential personnel who may be required to work on company holidays to maintain critical business operations. Essential roles typically include:

- IT operations and on-call engineers
- Customer support (Tier 1 and Tier 2)
- Facilities and security staff
- Any other roles designated by department heads with HR approval

### 6.2 Holiday Pay Rates

Essential personnel who work on an official company holiday receive:

- **1.5x their regular hourly rate** (or equivalent for salaried employees) for all hours worked on the holiday
- **Plus** one compensatory day off to be used within **90 days** of the worked holiday

Essential personnel schedules for holidays are communicated at least **30 days in advance**.

---

## 7. Holidays During PTO or Leave

- If an official company holiday falls during an employee's approved PTO period, the holiday is **not deducted** from the employee's PTO balance. (See PTO & Leave Policy, ACME-HR-001, Section 10.)
- Employees on FMLA leave, parental leave, or other approved leave during a holiday are still considered to be on leave; the holiday does not extend their leave period.
- Employees on unpaid leave are **not eligible** for holiday pay.

---

## 8. Part-Time Employee Holiday Eligibility

Part-time employees who are regularly scheduled to work on a day that falls on an official company holiday receive **holiday pay for their regularly scheduled hours** on that day.

Part-time employees receive **1 floating holiday** per calendar year (instead of 2 for full-time employees).

---

## 9. Holiday Schedule Changes

Acme Corp reserves the right to modify the holiday schedule with at least **60 days' notice** to employees. Any changes will be communicated via email and posted on the Acme HR Portal.
