# Acme Corp — New Employee Onboarding Guide

**Policy ID:** ACME-HR-008
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2026
**Approved By:** Sarah Chen, VP of Human Resources
**Applies To:** All newly hired employees of Acme Corp

---

## 1. Welcome to Acme Corp

Welcome to the team! This guide walks you through everything you need to know during your first 90 days at Acme Corp. Our onboarding program is designed to help you get up to speed quickly, build relationships with your colleagues, and set a strong foundation for your career here.

Your success matters to us. If you have any questions at any point during onboarding, don't hesitate to reach out to your manager, your onboarding buddy, or the HR team.

---

## 2. Before Your First Day

### 2.1 Pre-Boarding Checklist

Before you arrive, HR will send you the following items to complete:

- [ ] **Offer letter and employment agreement** — sign and return via DocuSign
- [ ] **Background check authorization** — complete online via our screening partner
- [ ] **Tax forms** (W-4, state withholding) — complete in the Acme HR Portal
- [ ] **Direct deposit setup** — provide banking information in the Acme HR Portal
- [ ] **Emergency contact information** — update in the Acme HR Portal
- [ ] **Benefits enrollment** — review plan options (you'll have 30 days from start date to enroll; see Benefits Overview, ACME-HR-007)
- [ ] **Equipment preferences** — indicate laptop preference (macOS or Windows) and any ergonomic needs

### 2.2 What We'll Have Ready for You

On your first day, the following will be set up and waiting:

- Company laptop (configured with required software and security tools)
- External monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset
- Google Workspace account (email, calendar, drive)
- Slack account with standard channel memberships
- Access badge for the office (if applicable)
- Welcome packet with Acme Corp swag

## 4. Your First Week

### 4.1 Week 1 Goals

By the end of your first week, you should:

- Understand your role, responsibilities, and initial objectives
- Have met your immediate team members and key cross-functional contacts
- Be set up on all necessary tools and systems
- Have completed all required Day 1 trainings
- Have your first 30-day goals documented with your manager

### 4.2 Key Activities

- **Manager 1:1**: Your manager will schedule a 1:1 meeting to discuss your role, team dynamics, expectations, and initial projects. This will become a weekly recurring meeting.
- **Team meetings**: Attend your team's regularly scheduled meetings (standups, planning, retrospectives).
- **Onboarding buddy meetings**: Your buddy will check in with you daily during Week 1 to answer questions and help you navigate.
- **HR check-in**: HR will reach out at the end of Week 1 to answer any questions about benefits, policies, or logistics.

### 4.3 Required Week 1 Trainings

| Training | Duration | Platform |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Security Awareness Fundamentals | 45 min | Acme Learning Portal |
| Data Privacy and Classification | 30 min | Acme Learning Portal |
| Acme Corp Product Overview | 60 min | Acme Learning Portal |
| Anti-Harassment Training | 30 min | Acme Learning Portal |
| Expense Policy Overview | 15 min | Acme Learning Portal |

## 5. Your First 30 Days

### 5.1 Goals

- Complete all required onboarding trainings
- Deliver your first meaningful contribution (as defined by your manager)
- Build relationships with key stakeholders outside your immediate team
- Understand the team's workflows, tools, and communication norms
- Have a clear understanding of how your work contributes to company goals

### 5.2 30-Day Check-In

At the end of your first 30 days, your manager will conduct a formal check-in to:

- Review your progress against initial goals
- Discuss what's going well and any challenges
- Adjust goals and expectations for the next 30 days
- Gather your feedback on the onboarding experience

---

## 6. Your First 60 Days

### 6.1 Goals

- Take ownership of one or more projects or workstreams
- Contribute independently with decreasing reliance on your onboarding buddy
- Participate in at least one cross-team collaboration or meeting
- Begin building expertise in your functional area

### 6.2 60-Day Check-In

Your manager will conduct a 60-day check-in to:

- Assess your ramp-up progress
- Discuss any skill gaps or additional training needs
- Set expectations for the remainder of the onboarding period

---

## 7. Your First 90 Days (Onboarding Completion)

### 7.1 Goals

- Operate independently in your role at a "Meets Expectations" level
- Have a solid understanding of Acme Corp's products, culture, and processes
- Complete all required onboarding trainings and compliance modules
- Successfully complete the 90-day onboarding period

### 7.2 90-Day Review

At the end of 90 days, your manager will conduct a formal **90-Day Review** that:

- Evaluates your performance during the onboarding period
- Confirms successful completion of the onboarding program
- Sets objectives for the remainder of the year
- Determines eligibility for hybrid/remote work arrangements (per Remote Work Policy, ACME-HR-002)
- Provides a forum for mutual feedback

**Note**: Successful completion of the 90-day onboarding period is required for continued employment. If performance concerns arise during onboarding, HR and the manager will work with the employee to create a focused improvement plan.

## 9. The Onboarding Buddy Program

### 9.1 What Is an Onboarding Buddy?

Every new employee is assigned an **onboarding buddy** — an experienced colleague from your team (or a closely related team) who serves as your go-to resource during your first 90 days.

### 9.2 Your Buddy Will Help You:

- Navigate the office, tools, and systems
- Understand team norms, unwritten rules, and culture
- Make introductions to key people across the organization
- Answer day-to-day questions that might feel "too small" to ask your manager
- Provide a safe space for honest questions about how things work

### 9.3 Buddy Meeting Schedule

| Period | Meeting Frequency |
|--------|-------------------|
| Week 1 | Daily (15 min) |
| Weeks 2–4 | 2x per week (30 min) |
| Weeks 5–12 | Weekly (30 min) |

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Payroll is processed semi-monthly on the 15th and the last day of each month via direct deposit.

**Q: How do I request time off?**
A: Submit PTO requests through the Acme HR Portal. See the PTO & Leave Policy (ACME-HR-001) for details.

**Q: Can I work remotely during onboarding?**
A: New employees are expected to work on-site during the 90-day onboarding period. Exceptions for specific circumstances may be arranged with your manager and HR.

**Q: What if I need help with my computer or software?**
A: Contact the IT Help Desk at helpdesk@acmecorp.com or extension x4800.

**Q: How do I set up my 401(k)?**
A: You are auto-enrolled at 6% after 30 days of employment. To change your contribution rate or investment selections, log into Fidelity NetBenefits at **netbenefits.fidelity.com**.

**Q: What's the dress code?**
A: Acme Corp has a business casual dress code. Use your judgment based on your role and any client interactions. When in doubt, ask your manager.
