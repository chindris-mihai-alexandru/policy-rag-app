## 1. Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive overview of the benefits available to Acme Corp employees. Acme Corp is committed to supporting the health, financial security, and professional growth of our team members.

## 2. Health Insurance

### 2.1 Medical Coverage

Acme Corp offers three medical plan options through Aetna:

| Plan | Employee Monthly Premium | Coverage Level | Deductible | Out-of-Pocket Max |
|------|------------------------|----------------|------------|-------------------|
| **PPO Standard** | $50 | 80/20 after deductible | $1,000 individual / $2,500 family | $5,000 / $10,000 |
| **PPO Premium** | $125 | 90/10 after deductible | $500 individual / $1,250 family | $3,000 / $6,000 |
| **HDHP + HSA** | $25 | 80/20 after deductible | $2,000 individual / $4,000 family | $6,000 / $12,000 |

**Acme Corp pays 80% of the premium** for all plans. The employee premiums listed above represent the employee's 20% share for individual coverage. Family coverage premiums are approximately 2.5x the individual rates.

### 2.2 Health Savings Account (HSA)

Employees enrolled in the HDHP plan are eligible for an HSA. Acme Corp contributes:

- **$750 annually** for individual HDHP enrollment
- **$1,500 annually** for family HDHP enrollment

Company HSA contributions are deposited quarterly. Employees may make additional pre-tax contributions up to the IRS annual limit ($4,300 individual / $8,550 family for 2026).

## 5. Retirement — 401(k) Plan

### 5.1 Plan Details

Acme Corp offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan through Fidelity Investments:

- **Eligibility**: All employees (full-time and part-time working 20+ hours/week) after **30 days of employment**
- **Employee contribution**: Up to the IRS annual limit ($23,500 for 2026; $31,000 for employees age 50+)
- **Company match**: Acme Corp matches **100% of the first 4%** of the employee's salary contributed to the plan
- **Vesting schedule**: Company matching contributions vest over **3 years** (1/3 per year of service)
- **Auto-enrollment**: New employees are automatically enrolled at a **6% contribution rate** into a target-date fund based on their expected retirement year. Employees may opt out or change their contribution rate at any time.

### 5.2 Investment Options

The 401(k) plan offers a diversified menu of investment options, including:

- Target-date retirement funds
- Index funds (S&P 500, total market, international, bond)
- Actively managed funds
- Company stock (Acme Corp stock available after IPO)
- Self-directed brokerage option for experienced investors

## 6. Life and Disability Insurance

### 6.1 Life Insurance

Acme Corp provides:

- **Basic life insurance**: **2x annual base salary** (up to $500,000), fully paid by Acme Corp
- **Supplemental life insurance**: Available at employee cost, up to an additional **5x annual salary** (up to $1,000,000); evidence of insurability may be required for amounts over $200,000
- **Spouse/domestic partner life insurance**: Available at employee cost, up to $100,000
- **Child life insurance**: $10,000 per child, available at employee cost

### 6.2 Short-Term Disability (STD)

- **Coverage**: 60% of base salary, up to $2,500/week
- **Waiting period**: 7 calendar days
- **Maximum duration**: 26 weeks
- **Cost**: Fully paid by Acme Corp

### 6.3 Long-Term Disability (LTD)

- **Coverage**: 60% of base salary, up to $10,000/month
- **Waiting period**: After exhaustion of STD benefits (26 weeks)
- **Maximum duration**: Until age 65 or Social Security Normal Retirement Age
- **Cost**: Fully paid by Acme Corp

## 8. Professional Development

### 8.1 Annual Budget

Each employee receives a **$2,000 annual professional development budget** for:

- Conference registration and attendance
- Online courses and certifications
- Workshops and seminars
- Professional books and subscriptions

### 8.2 Tuition Reimbursement

Full-time employees with at least **1 year of tenure** are eligible for tuition reimbursement:

- **Maximum**: $5,250 per calendar year (tax-free under IRS Section 127)
- **Eligible programs**: Degree programs, certificate programs, and individual courses related to the employee's current role or career development at Acme Corp
- **Grade requirement**: Grade of B or better (or "Pass" in pass/fail courses)
- **Commitment**: Employees must remain employed at Acme Corp for at least **12 months** after receiving tuition reimbursement or repay the full amount

### 8.3 Certification Support

Acme Corp will pay for job-related professional certifications, including:

- Exam registration fees
- Study materials (books, practice exams)
- Up to 2 exam attempts per certification

Eligible certifications include but are not limited to: AWS certifications, PMP, CPA, CISSP, PHR/SPHR, and other industry-recognized credentials approved by the employee's manager and HR.

## 11. Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)

### 11.1 Healthcare FSA

Employees enrolled in PPO plans may contribute up to **$3,200 per year** pre-tax to a Healthcare FSA for eligible medical, dental, and vision expenses not covered by insurance.

**Note**: Healthcare FSA is **not available** to employees enrolled in the HDHP + HSA plan (IRS rules).

### 11.2 Dependent Care FSA

All employees may contribute up to **$5,000 per year** ($2,500 if married filing separately) pre-tax to a Dependent Care FSA for eligible childcare or eldercare expenses.

### 11.3 FSA Use-It-or-Lose-It Rule

FSA funds must be used by **March 15** of the following year (2.5-month grace period). Unused funds after the grace period are forfeited per IRS rules.

## 13. Benefits Enrollment

### 13.1 New Hire Enrollment

New employees must enroll in benefits within **30 days** of their start date. If enrollment is not completed within this window, the employee will be enrolled in the PPO Standard medical plan and default dental and vision coverage.

### 13.2 Annual Open Enrollment

Open enrollment occurs annually in **November** for the following calendar year. During open enrollment, employees may:

- Add, remove, or change medical, dental, and vision plans
- Enroll in or modify FSA elections
- Update life insurance and disability coverage
- Add or remove dependents

### 13.3 Qualifying Life Events

Outside of open enrollment, benefit changes may only be made within **30 days** of a qualifying life event, such as:

- Marriage, divorce, or legal separation
- Birth or adoption of a child
- Loss of other coverage
- Change in employment status of spouse/domestic partner
