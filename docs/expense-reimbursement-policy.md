# Acme Corp — Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Policy ID:** ACME-FIN-001
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2026
**Approved By:** Michael Torres, VP of Finance
**Applies To:** All Acme Corp employees incurring business-related expenses

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the procedures and guidelines for reimbursement of legitimate business expenses incurred by Acme Corp employees. All employees are expected to exercise reasonable judgment and fiscal responsibility when incurring expenses on behalf of the company.

---

## 2. General Principles

- All expenses must be **business-related** and serve a legitimate company purpose.
- Employees must seek the **most cost-effective option** that meets business requirements.
- Expenses must be **pre-approved** when required by this policy.
- Original receipts (digital or physical) are required for all expenses **$25 and above**.
- Reimbursement requests must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of incurring the expense.
- Expenses older than **60 days** will not be reimbursed without VP-level approval.

---

## 3. Approval Thresholds

| Expense Amount | Approval Required |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Up to $100 | Direct manager |
| $101 – $500 | Direct manager + Department head |
| $501 – $2,500 | Department head + VP of Finance |
| Over $2,500 | VP of Finance + CFO |

**Pre-approval is required** for all anticipated expenses over **$500**. Submit a Pre-Approval Request via the Acme Expense Portal before incurring the expense.

---

## 4. Travel Expenses

### 4.1 Air Travel

- All air travel must be booked in **economy class** for domestic flights.
- **Business class** may be booked for international flights exceeding **6 hours** with pre-approval from the VP of Finance.
- Flights should be booked at least **14 days in advance** when possible to secure lower fares.
- Acme Corp's preferred airline is Delta Airlines; employees earn company rewards when booking through the Acme Travel Portal.

### 4.2 Ground Transportation

| Mode | Guideline |
|------|-----------|
| Rental car | Economy or compact class; pre-approved for trips where public transit is impractical |
| Rideshare (Uber/Lyft) | Permitted for airport transfers and local business travel; UberX/Lyft standard tier only |
| Personal vehicle | Reimbursed at the IRS standard mileage rate (**$0.70/mile** for 2026); requires mileage log |
| Parking & tolls | Reimbursable with receipts |
| Public transit | Fully reimbursable |

### 4.3 Lodging

- Hotel stays should not exceed **$200 per night** for domestic travel and **$300 per night** for international travel, excluding taxes and fees.
- Employees should use the Acme Travel Portal to book from preferred hotel partners.
- Extended stays (5+ nights) require pre-approval from the department head.
- Sharing accommodations is encouraged for team travel events to reduce costs.

### 4.4 Meals During Travel

Per-meal reimbursement limits during business travel:

| Meal | Domestic Limit | International Limit |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| Breakfast | $20 | $30 |
| Lunch | $30 | $40 |
| Dinner | $50 | $70 |

**Daily maximum**: $100 domestic / $140 international (exclusive of team dinners; see Section 5).

- Alcohol is **not reimbursable** during individual meals. Alcohol may be included in team dinner budgets at manager discretion (see Section 5.2).
- Tips are reimbursable up to **20%** of the pre-tax meal cost.

### 4.5 Incidentals

A daily incidental allowance of **$15** is provided for miscellaneous travel expenses (e.g., tips for porters, laundry for trips exceeding 3 nights). No receipts required for incidentals.

---

## 5. Meals and Entertainment

### 5.1 Business Meals (Non-Travel)

Meals with clients, partners, or candidates are reimbursable up to **$75 per person** with the following documentation:

- Names and titles of all attendees
- Business purpose of the meal
- Restaurant name and location
- Itemized receipt

### 5.2 Team Events and Dinners

- Team meals and events require **pre-approval** from the department head.
- Budget guideline: **$60 per person** for team dinners.
- Alcohol is permitted at team events at manager discretion, within the per-person budget.

### 5.3 Client Entertainment

Client entertainment expenses (e.g., event tickets, golf outings) require pre-approval from the VP of Finance and are limited to **$150 per client per event**.

---

## 6. Office and Work Supplies

### 6.1 Standard Office Supplies

Standard office supplies (pens, notebooks, paper, etc.) should be ordered through the Acme Office Manager. Direct purchases are reimbursable up to **$50 per quarter** without pre-approval.

### 6.2 Software and Subscriptions

- Software and subscription purchases must be pre-approved by IT and the employee's manager.
- Annual subscriptions over **$200** require VP approval.
- Acme Corp maintains enterprise licenses for standard tools; check with IT before purchasing individually.

### 6.3 Books and Learning Materials

Work-related books and learning materials are reimbursable up to **$200 per calendar year** without pre-approval. Requests exceeding this amount should be submitted through the Professional Development program (see Benefits Overview, ACME-HR-007).

---

## 7. Professional Development

Expenses for conferences, workshops, courses, and certifications are covered under the Acme Corp Professional Development Program:

- **Annual budget per employee**: $2,000
- Includes registration fees, travel, and accommodation
- Requires pre-approval from the employee's manager and HR
- See Benefits Overview (ACME-HR-007) for full details

- Monthly $75 internet and utilities stipend (all remote/hybrid employees)

These stipends are paid automatically and do not require expense reports.

## 9. How to Submit an Expense Report

### 9.1 Submission Process

1. Log into the **Acme Expense Portal** (expenses.acmecorp.com).
2. Create a new expense report with a descriptive title (e.g., "Q1 Sales Conference — Chicago").
3. Add each expense line item with:
   - Date of expense
   - Category (travel, meals, supplies, etc.)
   - Amount
   - Business purpose
   - Receipt (photo or PDF upload)
4. Submit for approval.

### 9.2 Approval Workflow

1. Direct manager reviews and approves/rejects within **5 business days**.
2. For amounts over $500, the report routes to additional approvers per Section 3.
3. Finance processes approved reports within **10 business days**.
4. Reimbursement is deposited via direct deposit on the next regular payroll cycle.

### 9.3 Rejected Expenses

If an expense is rejected, the employee will receive a notification with the reason. The employee may revise and resubmit the expense with additional documentation or justification.

## 10. Corporate Credit Cards

### 10.1 Eligibility

Corporate credit cards are issued to employees at the Director level and above, or to employees who incur frequent business expenses exceeding **$500 per month** on average.

### 10.2 Usage Rules

- Corporate cards are for **business expenses only**; personal use is strictly prohibited.
- All corporate card transactions must be reconciled in the Acme Expense Portal within **15 days** of the transaction date.
- Lost or stolen cards must be reported to Finance immediately at **finance@acmecorp.com** or extension **x4600**.

---

## 11. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are **not reimbursable** under any circumstances:

- Personal entertainment or recreation
- Traffic fines, parking tickets, or moving violations
- Airline upgrades (unless pre-approved for international business class per Section 4.1)
- Spouse or companion travel expenses
- Pet care or kennel fees during travel
- Personal clothing or grooming
- Gym memberships
- Political contributions
- Charitable donations (see Acme Corp community giving program separately)
- Co-working space memberships (unless pre-approved per Remote Work Policy)

---

## 12. Expense Fraud

Falsifying expense reports or submitting personal expenses as business expenses is considered **serious misconduct** and may result in:

- Immediate repayment of the fraudulent amount
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination
- Legal action if warranted

All expense reports are subject to random audit by the Finance department.
