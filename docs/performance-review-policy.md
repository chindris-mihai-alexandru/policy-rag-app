## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Acme Corp's performance management framework, including review cycles, rating criteria, feedback processes, performance improvement plans, and promotion guidelines. Our goal is to foster a culture of continuous feedback, professional growth, and accountability.

---

## 2. Performance Review Cycle

### 2.1 Annual Review

Acme Corp conducts a **formal annual performance review** in **January** each year, covering the previous calendar year (January 1 – December 31). The annual review includes:

- **Self-assessment**: Employee completes a written self-evaluation of goals achieved, key contributions, and areas for growth.
- **Peer feedback**: Each employee receives feedback from **3–5 peers** nominated by the employee and approved by the manager.
- **Manager assessment**: The manager evaluates the employee's performance against established goals and competencies.
- **Calibration**: Department-level calibration sessions ensure consistency and fairness in ratings across teams.
- **Review meeting**: The manager and employee meet to discuss the assessment, ratings, feedback, and development goals.

### 2.2 Mid-Year Check-In

A **formal mid-year check-in** is conducted in **July** to:

- Review progress on annual goals
- Identify any obstacles or support needed
- Adjust goals if business priorities have shifted
- Discuss career development aspirations

The mid-year check-in does **not** result in a formal rating but is documented in the Acme HR Portal.

### 2.3 Continuous Feedback

Beyond formal reviews, Acme Corp encourages **ongoing feedback** between managers and employees:

- **Weekly 1:1 meetings**: Required for all employees (see Remote Work Policy, ACME-HR-002)
- **Real-time feedback**: Managers and peers are encouraged to give timely, constructive feedback throughout the year
- **Recognition**: Acme Corp's peer recognition platform ("Acme Kudos") allows employees to publicly acknowledge colleagues' contributions

## 3. Performance Rating Scale

Employees receive one of the following ratings during the annual review:

| Rating | Label | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **5** | Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all goals and expectations; recognized as a top performer and role model; demonstrates extraordinary impact |
| **4** | Exceeds Expectations | Frequently exceeds goals; makes significant contributions beyond core responsibilities; demonstrates strong initiative |
| **3** | Meets Expectations | Consistently meets all goals and expectations; delivers reliable, quality work; demonstrates competence in role |
| **2** | Needs Improvement | Partially meets goals; some performance gaps identified; specific development areas require attention |
| **1** | Unsatisfactory | Fails to meet minimum expectations; significant performance deficiencies; immediate corrective action required |

### 3.1 Rating Distribution Guidelines

To maintain fairness and differentiation, Acme Corp uses the following **recommended distribution guidelines** (not rigid quotas):

| Rating | Recommended Distribution |
|--------|------------------------|
| 5 – Exceptional | ~5–10% of employees |
| 4 – Exceeds Expectations | ~20–30% |
| 3 – Meets Expectations | ~50–60% |
| 2 – Needs Improvement | ~5–10% |
| 1 – Unsatisfactory | ~0–5% |

These guidelines are reviewed annually by the leadership team and adjusted as needed based on organizational performance.

## 4. Goal Setting

### 4.1 SMART Goals

All employees set **3–5 SMART goals** at the beginning of each calendar year (or upon hire):

- **S**pecific: Clearly define what will be accomplished
- **M**easurable: Include quantifiable metrics or deliverables
- **A**chievable: Realistic given available resources and time
- **R**elevant: Aligned with team and company objectives
- **T**ime-bound: Include deadlines and milestones

### 4.2 Goal Alignment

Individual goals should align with:

1. **Team objectives**: Defined by the department head
2. **Company OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)**: Set quarterly by the leadership team
3. **Career development**: At least one goal should focus on professional growth

### 4.3 Goal Documentation

All goals are documented in the Acme HR Portal and visible to the employee and their manager. Goals may be adjusted mid-year with mutual agreement during the mid-year check-in.

## 5. Compensation and Performance

### 5.1 Merit Increases

Annual merit increases are tied to performance ratings:

| Rating | Typical Merit Increase Range |
|--------|------------------------------|
| 5 – Exceptional | 6–10% |
| 4 – Exceeds Expectations | 4–6% |
| 3 – Meets Expectations | 2–4% |
| 2 – Needs Improvement | 0% |
| 1 – Unsatisfactory | 0% |

Merit increases take effect on **April 1** following the annual review. The actual increase within each range depends on the employee's current position in their salary band, budget availability, and market data.

### 5.2 Bonuses

Eligible employees may receive annual performance bonuses based on individual performance and company financial results:

- **Target bonus**: Varies by level (typically 5–15% of base salary for individual contributors; 10–25% for managers and above)
- **Bonus multiplier**: Based on a combination of individual performance rating and company performance
- **Payout timing**: March of each year, covering the prior calendar year

### 5.3 Equity Refresh Grants

High-performing employees (rating of 4 or 5) may be eligible for annual equity refresh grants, subject to board approval and equity pool availability.

## 6. Promotions

### 6.1 Promotion Criteria

Promotions at Acme Corp are based on:

- **Sustained performance**: Demonstrated track record of exceeding expectations (minimum one annual review cycle at the current level with a rating of 4 or 5)
- **Readiness for next level**: The employee is already performing at the next level's expectations
- **Business need**: A role or scope increase exists to support the promotion
- **Manager recommendation**: The employee's manager sponsors the promotion through the calibration process

### 6.2 Promotion Process

1. The manager submits a promotion recommendation during the annual review calibration session.
2. The recommendation includes evidence of performance, scope expansion, and alignment with next-level expectations.
3. Department head and HR review and approve/deny the recommendation.
4. Approved promotions take effect on **April 1** alongside merit increases.
5. Promotions are accompanied by a **title change**, a **salary adjustment** to the new level's band, and updated responsibilities.

### 6.3 Promotion Salary Adjustments

Promoted employees receive a salary increase to bring them to at least the **minimum of the new level's salary band**, with a typical promotion increase of **10–15%** of base salary (in addition to any merit increase).

## 7. Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

### 7.1 When PIPs Are Used

A Performance Improvement Plan is initiated when an employee receives a rating of **2 (Needs Improvement)** or **1 (Unsatisfactory)**, or when significant performance concerns arise between review cycles.

### 7.2 PIP Components

Each PIP includes:

1. **Specific performance deficiencies**: Clear description of what is not meeting expectations
2. **Expected standards**: What "Meets Expectations" performance looks like for each deficiency
3. **Action plan**: Specific steps the employee must take, with measurable milestones
4. **Support provided**: Training, mentoring, reduced workload, or other resources the company will provide
5. **Timeline**: PIPs typically last **30, 60, or 90 days** depending on the severity and nature of the deficiency
6. **Check-in schedule**: Weekly meetings between the employee and manager to review progress
7. **Consequences**: Clear statement that failure to meet PIP requirements may result in demotion, reassignment, or termination

### 7.3 PIP Outcomes

At the conclusion of the PIP:

- **Successful completion**: The PIP is closed, and the employee returns to normal performance management. A note is placed in the employee's file.
- **Partial improvement**: The PIP may be extended for an additional 30 days (maximum one extension).
- **Failure to improve**: The employee may be terminated, demoted, or reassigned, at the discretion of the manager, HR, and department head.

### 7.4 Employee Rights During PIP

- Employees on a PIP continue to receive their full salary and benefits.
- Employees may request a meeting with HR to discuss any concerns about the PIP process.
- PIPs are not retaliatory; they are designed to provide a structured path back to good standing.

## 8. Feedback Best Practices

### 8.1 For Managers

- Provide specific, actionable feedback (not vague generalizations)
- Balance positive recognition with constructive suggestions
- Document feedback in the Acme HR Portal for reference during reviews
- Address performance issues promptly — don't wait for the annual review
- Seek to understand the employee's perspective before drawing conclusions

### 8.2 For Employees

- Actively seek feedback from your manager and peers
- Approach feedback with a growth mindset
- Document your accomplishments and contributions throughout the year for self-assessment
- Raise concerns about goals or expectations early, before they become performance issues

## 10. Confidentiality

Performance reviews, ratings, and related documentation are **confidential**. Access is limited to:

- The employee
- The employee's direct manager and skip-level manager
- HR personnel involved in the review process
- Senior leadership during calibration sessions

Employees should not share their performance ratings or compensation details with colleagues, except in the context of protected concerted activity under applicable labor law.

---

## 11. Appeals Process

Employees who disagree with their annual performance rating may submit a **written appeal** to HR within **15 business days** of their review meeting. The appeal should include:

- The specific rating being contested
- Evidence supporting the employee's position
- Requested resolution

HR will review the appeal with the employee's skip-level manager and respond within **15 business days**.
